Privacy preview

Here’s how ShotBrief handles screenshots in the current pre-release version. The full privacy policy will be published before the app is released.

What stays on your iPhone

ShotBrief reads text and QR codes on your iPhone. It stores imported images, recognized text, results, edits and saved links in protected local storage. The library is excluded from device backups and does not sync across devices. ShotBrief has no account.

When an image or text goes to Google

If you allow cloud analysis, ShotBrief sends recognized text, including decoded QR contents, through Firebase AI Logic to Google Gemini for summaries and suggested actions. When text is insufficient, it also sends a compressed image. Google receives technical information used to operate and protect the service. It may temporarily retain submitted content for abuse prevention and process it outside the EEA.

ShotBrief can continue with local text recognition and manual review if you decline. You can withdraw consent from the Inbox menu under Privacy. This stops future cloud requests and cannot recall content Google already received.

What happens when you take an action

Contacts and calendar entries are saved through system editors. The account you choose may sync them. Links open in your browser. If you delete a Photos original, iOS asks you to confirm. Clearing the Inbox keeps your library. Removing the app removes its local library.

Visiting this site

The example picker runs in your browser and uses fictional examples. It has no upload field or analytics. GitHub Pages hosts this site and logs visitor IP addresses for security. GitHub Pages data collection.
