ShotBrief support

Get help with screenshot review, search or cloud analysis.

Import a screenshot

Open Inbox and tap + to select up to 20 images. Open a suggested action to review its details before you save.

Find a previous scan

In Search or Collections, choose History and saved links. Search can also look through photos you’ve allowed ShotBrief to access.

Change your cloud choice

Open the Inbox menu and choose Privacy to review or withdraw consent. Local text recognition and manual review remain available.

Manage original photos

Clearing the Inbox queue keeps the library. When Delete is available for a Photos original, iOS asks you to confirm before deleting it. ShotBrief keeps the scan history.
